## Verdict

**APPROVE WITH COMMENTS.** There is no Blocker or Critical finding, so the §9 item 2 merge condition is met.

- **Security holds.** The Admin is the only caller at the manifest, the route and the service, and 401/403 come before any read. The password goes only to `verifyStepUp`, after every cheap refusal, on the shared step-up throttle. No snapshot, formula or rate reaches any answer, and nothing reaches an Engineer.
- **Data integrity holds.** The revert stores a copy of a stored version through the one guarded writer, with its audit entry in one transaction. Two reverts, or a revert and any other configuration write, cannot both win (proven against PostgreSQL). No estimate data changes, and approved estimates stay on their pinned version. Checkpoints are immutable, the names are unique ignoring case, and the backfill and the seed step are correct and tested.
- **Recommended before the squash:** **SQR-01 (Major)**, a few lines plus one route test. If it is not done here, it goes to a `tech-debt` issue for the hardening window (not waived).
- **Follow-ups:** SQR-02 added to #524 (hardening window). SQR-04 to reviewer-performance. SQR-05 and SQR-07 to the ux-expert and product-owner. SQR-08 to the solution-architect and ux-expert. SQR-03 and SQR-06 optional.
