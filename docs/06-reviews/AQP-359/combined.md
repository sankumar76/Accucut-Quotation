## Verdict

**APPROVE WITH COMMENTS.** There are no Blocker, Critical or Major findings, and **nothing must be fixed before merge.**

- **Security holds.** The Admin is the only caller at the manifest, the route and the service, and 403 and 401 are decided before anything is read. The password goes only to `verifyStepUp`, comes after the cheap refusals, and uses the shared step-up throttle. Nothing reaches an Engineer, and the 409 body is rebuilt key by key.
- **Data integrity holds.** A protected or referenced field cannot be deleted. Two layers stop it, and every lookup the code depends on names a protected field. Each delete re-reads the references and writes through the guarded insert, so two deletes, or a delete and a save, cannot both win. That is proven against PostgreSQL with deterministic race tests. The version and its audit entry are atomic. Re-lettering rewrites no formula and keeps every value.
- **Follow-ups:**
  - **SQR-01 (Minor):** a cheap widening of the guard test, for the hardening window (a `tech-debt` issue if master defers it).
  - **SQR-03:** solution-architect.
  - **SQR-02, SQR-05 and SQR-06:** ux-expert and product-owner at the end review.
  - **SQR-04:** noted for the product-owner.
