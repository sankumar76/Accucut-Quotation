How it works at each size

  • 1024 px and wider: persistent graphite side menu, 248 px.
  • 768 to 1023 px (tablet): the side menu collapses; the menu button in the top bar opens it as a drawer. The bell stays visible.
  • Up to 767 px (phone): same drawer, 288 px wide, opened from the menu button; the bell and the account menu stay in the top bar. See the phone frame with the drawer open.
  • The account menu (open on this page) shows name, e-mail and role, then Change password and Sign out.

Review note. First-open states (UX spec version 0.11, section 7.3.1 rule 11 and SCR-25; AQP-188). The review bar switches the live top bar between the account trigger pending and failed. The menu button is hidden at 1024 px and wider, so its states show below that width: narrow the window, or open the phone frame. In the product the failure line is added when the load fails and is announced by role="alert"; here it is shown and hidden by the state buttons.
