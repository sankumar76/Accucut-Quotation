## Way of working assumed (condition 3 of G4 attempt 2; release plan v0.12 section 3.3, DL-041)

**The way of working is way 5 (release plan 3.3; chosen by the delivery lead on 2026-11-09, DL-118): after master gives QA (G7) a GO, the orchestrator squash-merges the story's code pull request itself, once its required checks are green on the head G7 judged and the docs PR it depends on has merged (CLAUDE.md section 8, `MERGE_POLICY` all-auto). Deployment, releases and G8 stay with the delivery lead. This section names it, which is G4 condition 3 (DL-118 (5): "scrum-master names the way in `sprint-01-plan.md`"); it was open and overdue until this version (0.12). Until 2026-11-09 the plan assumed way 1 (one pull request per story, merged by the delivery lead within half a working day); the bullets below that describe way 1 are kept as history and no longer apply.**

- Each story has its own branch `feat/<JIRA-KEY>-<slug>` from `master` and its own PR. A story that depends on another starts its branch **after** the earlier one is merged (no stacked branches, no bundled PRs).
- **Superseded by DL-118 (2026-11-09):** code PRs were to be merged by a human (`MERGE_POLICY`: docs-auto, code-human). Under way 5 the orchestrator merges them after G7 GO (DL-118 (3)); every pre-merge condition in a gate record still binds (for example a docs PR first). The branch rule above stands: a story that depends on another starts its branch after the earlier one has merged, and under way 5 that merge follows G7 GO at once, with no wait for the delivery lead. The product-manager re-runs the dates without a turnaround (M); this plan adopts no date.
- Why this matters: sprint 1's longest chain is 8 to 9 stories, so it has 7 to 8 merge waits. The product-manager's model puts sprint 1 at about 5.85 days for 2.4 days of work at a half-day merge, and the merge turnaround is the largest input to every date (release plan v0.12). From 2026-11-04 the two enablers (AQP-169, AQP-188) stand in series before AQP-48, so the chain has two more merge waits; the product-manager's upper-bound arithmetic puts the central date at Fri 2026-11-27 and the high end at Wed 2026-12-16 (release plan v0.15 3.8; model not re-run). **No date is adopted.** The 54-point commitment (53 until 2026-11-04) and the 55-point capacity are stated without a velocity (above) and do not allow for a slower merge than half a day.
- **Closed 2026-11-09 (DL-118): way 5 is chosen.** The capacity row is unchanged (it never allowed for a slower merge than half a day, and way 5 is faster). History: the other ways, for the delivery lead to choose by name (release plan 3.3): 2 fixed merge times twice a day; 3 stacked branches; 4 bundled PRs; 5 the orchestrator merges after G7 GO. If they name one, or a different turnaround (for example a whole day), this section and the capacity row change at the next sprint plan; silence leaves way 1 at half a day and is not a stop (condition 2, orchestrator).
