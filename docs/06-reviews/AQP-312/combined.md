| SQR-01 | **Major** | `deploy/windows/AccucutOps.psm1:2951` (the safety copy) → `Invoke-AccucutBackup` → `:2145` `Invoke-AccucutBackupPrune` → `Get-AccucutBackupPruneCandidate` (`:1816`) | **The safety copy can delete the copy the Admin is restoring.** The safety copy is an ordinary `Invoke-AccucutBackup` run, and every such run ends with the weekly prune rule: it deletes Verified copies older than 2 calendar years that are not among the newest 4, and the new safety copy pushes one more copy out of those 4. ## AC-8 kit: is it usable by Accucut IT?

**Yes, with two small changes.** It is short (6 steps, about 15 minutes) and self-contained. It is safe: it uses a made-up value, never touches the install, and cleans up everything it made. It mirrors the real endpoint's shape (`RestrictedRemoteServer`, a virtual account in one group, a script file importing a module by path, `Read-Host` and `Read-Host -AsSecureString`). Its result is unambiguous: six PASS lines and one verdict line, plus a clear "stop and report" rule. Its secret check hashes the value and does not echo it. The two changes, both in SQR-06:

1. Add the optional standard-account connection, so the kit also covers A-35 and not just the prompts.
2. Ship it in the zip, or tell IT where to download it, and send the result to the delivery lead rather than a Jira comment.

A FAIL caused by the kit's own extra cmdlets (`Set-Content`, `ConvertTo-Json`) would only cause a needless escalation, never a false PASS, which is the safe direction.
