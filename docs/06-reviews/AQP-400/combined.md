## Summary
The security design is sound and is built the way architecture section 4.12 c asks. An Engineer is refused 403 by `withRoute` before the route's params are parsed. The service checks the role again before it opens a transaction. The write is one transaction under the estimate's `FOR UPDATE` lock. Inside it, the Ignored layer is refused explicitly before the state machine is asked. The status, `quoted_at`, `quoted_by` and the `estimate.quoted` entry are written together, and the 200 answer is a strict `{status, quoted:{at, byName}}`. There is no `UNMARK_QUOTED` event, route or button. Unlock and Cancel from QUOTED go to Draft and clear the marker in the same UPDATE. The Lead-only data (the `quoted` marker, the Downloaded chip) never reaches the Engineer's branch of the page or any schema an Engineer is answered with. The tests are thorough at every layer. They include a real row-lock wait, two presses at once, and an audit failure that rolls the mark back.

One Major finding, test-only: the second `test.slow()` in the new E2E spec was added after one local cold-server flake, with no measurement and no diagnosis. DL-229 (4)(b) and DL-231 (2) forbid that, and CI shows the test does not need it (QLT-01). Three Minor findings: the other `test.slow()` needs its measured figures recorded, or the test should be split (QLT-02); a refusal sentence and focus are lost when the page redraws (QLT-03); and the dialog's refusal and no-answer branches have no test (QLT-04). The first-load deltas (+12 B on `/queue`, +198 B on `/estimates/[code]`) were checked against the CI logs and are accepted (QLT-05).
