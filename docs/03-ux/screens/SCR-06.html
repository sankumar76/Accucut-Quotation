How the total is built

Show

Every level's quantity applies. Each row's cost (column BG) already includes that row's own quantity. The total adds each row's BG times the quantities of all the rows above it, and a row's own quantity is never applied a second time. For example, 2 frames with 3 pipes each and 4 lids on each pipe count 2 × 3 × 4 = 24 lids, 6 pipes and 2 frames. If one lid costs 1 and one pipe costs 1, and the frame row itself holds nothing, the lid row shows 4, the pipe row shows 3 + 3 × 4 = 15, and the frame, which is the item, shows 2 × 15 = 30.

In this estimate, row 1.1.1 counts 2 × 2 × 4 = 16 pieces: 2 control cabinet assemblies, 2 base frames in each and 4 corner gussets on each frame.
