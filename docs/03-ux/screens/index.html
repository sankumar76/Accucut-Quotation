Screens for review (G3)

Review mock-ups, not production code. Developers rebuild them with shadcn/ui. All names, part numbers, rates and prices are invented. Version 0.1, 2026-11-01. Specification: docs/03-ux/ux-spec.md.

How to review

  • Open any page by double-click. Nothing needs a server, a build or a sign-in.
  • The yellow bar at the top of each screen is review-only. Its buttons switch the screen between its states: default, loading, empty, error, success, and the dialogs. Links in the table below open a state directly.
  • Phone (360 px) and tablet (768 px) links open the real screen inside a device-sized frame. You can also narrow your browser window on any screen.
  • Buttons and links between screens click through the main journeys: queue, estimate, cost details, approve, export.
  • Engineer screens (SCR-03 Engineer, SCR-05, SCR-23, SCR-24 and the Engineer's e-mails) show no cost, rate or formula anywhere.
