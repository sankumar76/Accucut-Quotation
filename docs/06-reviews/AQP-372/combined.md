## Verdict

**APPROVE WITH COMMENTS.** There are no Blocker, Critical or Major findings, so the §9 item 2 merge condition is met.

- **Engineer confidentiality holds (NFR-002).** `isOnForm` still checks the flag, the allow-list and the field kind for every field in every section before the new-section or choice paths. The form answer is strict and carries only option words. The canary test with the worst stored registry finds nothing for any role.
- **Server validation holds.** A choice is checked against the server's own option list, and a field not on the form is refused. A value outside the list is accepted only when it is exactly the value already stored (AC-2).
- **Data integrity holds.** Section numbers are the next after the highest, unique and race-safe through the guarded writer. The section and field are one version with their audit entry in one transaction. The audit change reads old entries unchanged. Approved estimates stay on their own version.
- **Optional before the squash:** SQR-01 and SQR-02 (Minor, a few lines each). If not done here, they go to the hardening window.
- **Follow-ups:** SQR-03 for any future option editor or import story. SQR-04 and SQR-06 to the ux-expert, product-owner and solution-architect. SQR-05 to QA at the end review.
