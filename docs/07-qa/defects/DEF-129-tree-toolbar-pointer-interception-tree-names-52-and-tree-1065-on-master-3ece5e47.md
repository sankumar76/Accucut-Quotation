## Evidence (job log, ANSI stripped; no secrets or personal data)
- **Mechanism, master (DL-426 (3)).** While the toolbar is clipped to 1 px, Playwright aims at the button's point, which lies over the next row; that row takes the pointer and its own hover, so the toolbar the test wants never opens, and the click retries until the budget runs out. A person cannot aim at a toolbar they cannot see: they hover the row, the toolbar line opens under it, and they move down onto it. So the mechanism is the test's order of actions, made timing-dependent by the row shift the design accepts.
- **Not DEF-085.** Neither failure shows `waiting for locator('form[data-part-form]')`; DEF-085's 4 misses on this push are `estimate-form-choice.spec.ts` :88, :109, :126 and `estimate-form-sections.spec.ts:64` (QA's grep of the whole CI log: that signature appears only under those four, three times each). DL-425 (4) counted `estimate-tree-names:52` among them; DL-426 (A) corrects it.
- **Not part of this defect:** `estimate-tree.spec.ts:790` [mobile] (shard 1, no. 354, 29.4 s, passed on retry): `expect(getByRole('alertdialog', { name: /^Delete 1\.1 .+\?$/ })).toContainText(...)` ran out on its own 15 s after Enter (spec :890), `element(s) not found`; no pointer interception in shard 1. ## Product fault measured by lane 2: the hover toolbar moves the rows below it (2026-11-27; test plan 0.3.92)
**Lane 2's measurement (relayed by the orchestrator, 2026-11-27; QA did not run it) on `master` `d2b58f04`, real Chromium, 1366x768, fine pointer, a 5-row tree:** hovering a row shows its toolbar line (**38 px plus a 4 px margin**), which **pushes every row below it down 42 px**. Moving the pointer from row 1.1 (toolbar shown) **straight down onto row 1.2's link, 1.2's link slides 42 px up, above the pointer, and the pointer lands on 1.2's own toolbar: `elementFromPoint` returns 'Add fastener under 1.2'** (a real user would press the wrong row's button). With a still pointer and a visible toolbar the buttons are hit correctly. **This is the product fault DL-426 named:** the ruling's check ('moving down onto a row's toolbar keeps it', 'one toolbar at a time with no flicker') passed on `744a8fa5` with a slow 3 px pointer, and **the measurement with a normal pointer shows the rows moving under it, so the hand check fails.** **Consequence under DL-426 (3): DEF-129 becomes a provisional Sev-2, type Product, the same day, and comes to master; tree PRs hold until it is fixed** (master's ruling on the grade, the hold and on **whether DEF-129 and DEF-132 are merged into one record** is pending, DL number to come). The interceptions of the CI cases (`estimate-tree-names.spec.ts:52`, `estimate-tree.spec.ts:901` and `:1065`) are this fault seen by Playwright (the pointer aimed at the toolbar button's point, which the row hover has moved), at fast draws too (0.65, PR #803's run). **The ux-expert is designing a toolbar that never moves the rows** (the design ruling to come); the fix then is the product's, and the hover helper of #796 would make the tests pass without removing the fault, so QA asks that the proof be on the product behaviour: **a pointer moved at a normal speed from a row down onto the next row's link always lands on that link (no row moves under it), the toolbar buttons are hit by a still pointer, no layout shift on hover (CLS 0 for the hover), and the existing cases pass without a helper that waits for the toolbar.** The hand check (DL-426) is repeated after the fix by lane 2 and by the ux-expert.
